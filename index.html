Built for the second coffee, and the tenth

  1. Say what you are after

    Not just a hobby list. Tell us your situation and the kind of friendship you want.

  2. Meet your small circle

    We suggest a time and a public place with 2 to 4 people who want the same thing.

  3. Meet again

    A week later we check in. If it clicked, we plan the next one for the same people, or you can meet someone new.

  4. Belong here

    Friends stay in touch, welcome the next newcomer and make the town feel like yours.

Not just another way to meet new people

Friends, not contacts

The goal is people who chat, ask about your week and turn up for you. A one-off meetup is only the start.

Keep the ones who click

Friendships take repeat time together, so we help you meet again with the people you connect with. No one is stuck, and you can always meet someone new.

Everyone wants the same thing

Everyone who joins says they are looking for lasting friendship and happy to meet more than once.

Welcome in a new place

Matched on where you are in life, such as new here, new parent or newly retired, so people understand what you are going through.

Who it is for

Anyone who wants someone to ask how they got on, not just someone to sit with once.

  • New to the area
  • Working from home
  • Recently retired
  • New parents
  • Carers
  • Practising English
  • Moved for work or study
  • After a big life change
  • Shy, and ready to try

Meeting new people, kept simple

Public places only

Cafés, parks and community spaces. Never someone's home.

Small groups

Two to four people, so there is no pressure and no awkward silence to fill alone.

Your details stay private

Matches see your first name. Your email is never shared.

You are in control

Decline any invitation, and block or report anyone, at any time.
